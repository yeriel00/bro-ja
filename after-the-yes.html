After the Yes

A clear path from yes to treatment

A “yes” opens the path to diagnosis. You can start and manage migraine care in frontline practice, and we outline what comes next across acute care and outpatient clinics.

FPO

Three steps after the yes

None of it has to happen in one visit. Each step can be spread across follow-ups.

Only have two minutes?

Share a provisional diagnosis and let your patient know treatment is available. A patient guide can help them get started until further workup.

Guidance for the patients you see

Migraine presents differently across populations and over a lifetime, and treatment shifts with it. We recommend annual screening from adolescence through menopause to track changes.1

  • Hormones, contraception, and pregnancy

    Navigate menstrual migraine, aura and estrogen use, and care through pregnancy, postpartum, and menopause.

    • See Guidance and Resources

  • Children and teens

    See how migraine presents in younger patients, treatment options and school support resources.

    • See Guidance and Resources

  • Older adults

    Evaluate new headache after 50 and choose treatment that fits alongside other medications and comorbidities.

    • See Guidance and Resources
