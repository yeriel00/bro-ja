Why Ask

The case for asking

Migraine takes a heavy toll on work, family, and daily life, yet it often goes undiagnosed and untreated. One routine question can change that.

Question — The Ask

The Ask

One question to help identify patients with migraine

Developed with input from frontline clinicians, this question is designed to fit into routine visits and uncover headaches that interfere with daily life.

“In the past month, has a headache interfered with your work, school, family, or other activities?”
