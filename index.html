More than 40 million Americans live with migraine1

Fewer than 1 in 3 have sought care, been diagnosed, and are taking recommended acute medication.2

Led by:

FPO

Better migraine care starts with asking

Migraine is diagnosable and treatable, yet the World Health Organization ranks it a leading cause of disability worldwide.3 Asking about how headaches affect daily life can help identify patients who may benefit from diagnosis and effective treatment.

See the Question

Just Ask

One question that fits into every visit

Patients may keep working, caring for family, and going to school despite disabling symptoms. This brief question focuses on what headaches interfere with and can be added to intake, rooming, or a routine visit.

Simple by Design

A disability that can hide in plain sight

Migraine is a neurological disease that can affect work, family, and everyday activities. Some patients assume their symptoms are simply part of life or do not realize effective treatment is available.

FPO

More ways to treat migraine

Migraine treatment has advanced significantly in recent years, with new options for acute and preventive care. Identifying patients with migraine is the first step toward finding a treatment plan that fits their needs.
