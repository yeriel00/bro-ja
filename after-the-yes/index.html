After the Yes

What to do after a patient says yes

A “yes” opens the path to diagnosis. Migraine can often be effectively treated and managed in frontline practice. These practical resources guide you through evaluation, treatment, and follow-up.

FPO

Three steps after the yes

Start with the step your patient needs today. Assessment, treatment planning, and follow-up can take place across visits when clinically appropriate.

Only have two minutes?

Share a preliminary assessment, let your patient know treatment options are available, and arrange a follow-up for further evaluation.

Guidance for different patient needs

Migraine presents differently across populations and over a lifetime, and treatment shifts with it. We recommend annual screening from adolescence through menopause to track changes.1

  • Hormones, contraception, and pregnancy

    Navigate menstrual migraine, aura and estrogen use, and care through pregnancy, postpartum, and menopause.
